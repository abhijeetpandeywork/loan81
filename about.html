What We Do

At Loan81, we act as loan advisors and loan assistance professionals. Our role is to understand your requirements and help facilitate your journey towards suitable loan options available through our lending and financial-service partners.

Depending on your requirements, we assist with:

Understanding different loan options
Identifying potentially suitable lending options
Understanding basic eligibility requirements
Guiding customers through enquiry and application
Assisting with documentation requirements
Facilitating communication with relevant lenders
Our objective is simple: make the loan journey easier, clearer, and more convenient for our customers.

We Are Advisors, Not Lenders

It is important to understand our role clearly. Loan81 is not a bank, NBFC, or direct lending institution.

Loan81 works as a loan advisory and assistance platform. We do not directly provide or disburse loans.

The final decision regarding whether a loan is approved, along with the sanctioned loan amount, interest rate, tenure, fees, documentation, and other terms, is made strictly by the respective bank, NBFC, financial institution, or lender based on its own policies and eligibility criteria.
