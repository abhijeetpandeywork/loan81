Hassle-Free Preparation

Basic Eligibility & Required Documents

Keep these essential documents ready to expedite your loan evaluation and increase the chances of quick lender sanction.

KYC & Personal Identity

  • PAN Card (Mandatory for all financial transactions)
  • Aadhaar Card (Linked with active mobile number for e-KYC)
  • Current Address Proof (Passport, Voter ID, Utility Bill, or Rental Agreement)
  • Passport size recent photographs

Income & Banking Verification

  • Latest 3 to 6 Months Salary Slips with official company stamp
  • Latest 6 Months Salary Bank Account Statement (PDF format)
  • Form 16 or Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 1-2 years
  • Company ID Card / Offer Letter (for corporate verification)

Business & Personal KYC

  • PAN Card of Individual Applicant and Entity/Firm
  • Aadhaar Card of Proprietor / Partners / Directors
  • Business Proof (GST Registration, MSME Certificate, Shop & Est. License)
  • Partnership Deed or MOA/AOA (if applicable)

Financial Records & Statements

  • Last 2-3 Years Income Tax Returns (ITR) with Computation of Income
  • Audited Balance Sheet & Profit & Loss Statement (for audits)
  • Last 12 Months Current Account Bank Statement
  • GST Return Filings (GSTR-3B) for the latest 12 months
